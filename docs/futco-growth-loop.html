The FutCo Growth Loop

One cycle, run per product (OppScan first). Corp watches real ad, revenue, and traffic signals, decides what's broken, commissions creative or hands a genuine capability gap to Pilot, executes within human-set spend bounds, and measures what really happened before running it again — with a parallel, human-gated channel finding and drafting organic engagement for e3d.ai on X.

Ad-spend guardrails

e3d-corp can submit or tighten a capital mandate against e3d-trade's own autonomous trading limits (position/exposure/turnover) — it can only tighten them, never loosen; e3d-trade reports outcomes back via its own webhook, which Measure/Learn above fold in
